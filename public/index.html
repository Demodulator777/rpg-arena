Why bother building another synth?

Because most free synths are either a demo of a paid one or a wall of oscillators nobody mapped properly. Morpho is one complete instrument, finished and documented.

FM

Audio-rate FM matrix

A full 4×4 modulation matrix routing between all four operators at audio rate. Stack operators for glass, grit, and harmonics that move with the note rather than sliding underneath it.

▚

Wavetables from your own audio

Drop a WAV, AIFF, FLAC or MP3 onto the display and Morpho builds a wavetable out of it, with four conversion modes and four normalisation curves to choose from.

◎

Mipmapped and phase-aligned

Tables are pre-baked into mip levels so high notes stay clean instead of aliasing, and frames are phase-aligned so morph sweeps do not click when you move between them.

⇄

Sixteen modulation routes

Four LFOs, four envelopes, four macros, MIDI CC and note tracking — all assignable across sixteen slots with the amount dialled in exactly where you want it.

▤

A real preset bank

Factory patches ship with the plugin, your own presets save with their wavetable embedded, and there are favourites, categories and instant search so you stop scrolling.

♪

Effects already in the box

Seven effect units in a chain, two filters in series, and a standalone build you can run without opening your DAW at all.

Get it in three moves

The whole thing is a folder — nothing installs, nothing phones home.

  1. Download the bundle

    Grab the ZIP from the plugin page. It contains the VST3 folder and a standalone Morpho.exe.

  2. Drop the folder into your VST3 path

    Each `.vst3` is a folder — copy the whole folder, Contents and all, into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\. Do not open it or move only the binary inside it.

  3. Rescan and play

    Restart your DAW, refresh the plugin list, and look under instruments or effects as appropriate.
